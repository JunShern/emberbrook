Cut-in scale & alignment

Every frame below is the real game — play3d.html at 1920×1080 over the baked emb-cine plate, driven through Dialogue.play(). Numbers are measured on the live element, not computed from the file.

The bug

The layout gave every cut-in the same element height and let the plate's aspect decide its width. That normalises the PNG, not the character in it — and the plates are not framed alike. A head spans 0.26 of Boatwright's plate and 0.71 of Mara's. At one shared height that is a 2.73× spread in face size, which is what the user saw: "the cat looks huge relative to the humans".

The fix

Scale on the head, not the bounds. Each character carries a hand-marked head box (crown / eye / chin, as fractions of its own plate height) in public/assets/characters/cutins.json. The element height is solved backwards from a target head height, so the plate's aspect and framing become consequences rather than inputs. Mochi carries hscale: 0.86 — the one hand-set number, because a cat's head is not a person's head and matching them exactly is only less wrong than matching the images.

Align on the eyeline. The old datum was the element's bottom, which aligns the crops; once heads are equal it scatters faces by whatever torso each plate happens to include. The eye is what the player's own eye goes to, and because heads are now one size it drags the chins into line for free.

What is still wrong, and why it is not a constant

Mara sits 41 px low and Mochi 16 px low. Both plates are near head-and-shoulders crops (head 0.71 and 0.53 of the image, against a cast median of 0.34), so once their heads are scaled down to the cast's size there is not enough body left under the face to reach the dialogue box from the shared eyeline. Rather than hang a straight cut edge in mid-air, those two drop until they sink. The fix is a re-crop of those two plates, not another number in the layout — tools/cutin_edge.py already flags Mara's framing as out-of-family.

Instruments: tools/cutin_headbox.py (--sheets to mark from, --verify to check the marks against every mood plate, --report for the shipped layout on paper — it agrees with the browser to within 2 px). Marks verified against every mood in docs/qa/cutins/headbox/verify/.
