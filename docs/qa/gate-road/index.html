Why it read as a plaza — the one measurement that explains everything

gate_build lays the carriageway on the walk graph: road_at() asks walk_ref() for the nearest walk record and paves everything within ROAD_W + 0.45 of it. walk_lm_porters-yard was an 8 × 8 m filled disc of walk. A disc of walk paves a disc of road. Projecting the shipped gate_road mesh through the solved camera: 2 921 upward-facing quads covering the bottom-right third of the plate.

So the fix was a map edit plus a re-derive, not new art. Delete the disc and the same builder lays a lane. Measured carriageway width, per metre of x, from the pass's own manifest:

The bulge at the mouth (x 0..3) is the exit's own 2.6 m threshold pad; past it the spine tapers to 3.4 m as the road leaves the parcel. Before, the same measure ran to the full 11 m width of the tier.

Honest reds

  • The gorge face reads as smooth dunes, not rock, in this frame. It is the object another lane built today; the chop exposes more of it and the new west end is steeper (fall range 24 – 71° against 24 – 33° before). It is the largest single shape in the frame's left half and it is the weakest thing in the picture. Not fixed here — that is the gorge lane's object and its relief model, and a red-team pass on it is a separate ask.
  • The mouth is 6.2 m wide where the exit's threshold pad sits, against 3.4 m on the spine past it. Two-thirds of that bulge is below the frame edge, but if the road should be one width all the way out, the pad has to stop paving — that is a change to road_at's admission rule and it wants a ruling, not a guess.
  • charPxMin 45 → 44 on this shot. The ratchet fired exactly as designed and the argument is written into dellhollow.cameras.json: the owned hull's west lobe went from an 8 m disc to a 2.6 m pad 1.7 m nearer the lens, the camera stood back 1.47 m to hold it, and the far end of the winch road lost one pixel. The player reads 104 px where the road puts her.
