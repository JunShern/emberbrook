Overworld gauntlet loop

A blind-critic loop on the overworld's art. Each round: builders change something, a FRESH Opus 5 critic that has never seen this project compares our frames against real FFIX-reimagined screenshots without being told which is which, and its reasons become the next round's work. The bar is artistry, not numeric similarity. NOTE ON VIEWPOINTS: rounds 0-3 shot low corridor/closeup views; round 4 switched to the four standing views (meadow/gate/gorge/vista) and no instrument caught the change — the user did, from this page. The FOLIAGE loop below therefore shoots FIVE fixed views including the restored low closeup, and they do not change again.

Round 4 — The grade goes into the light; the artefacts get named

2026-08-04 04:30 · 4/4 frames captured

Critic verdict: Ranked 4th of 4. The critic's headline: 'There is not one cast shadow in the entire frame… the houses have no lit side and no shade side. Four walls, one colour.' And its summary of two frames from the same build: 'A got a colour grade and B got a light.' It ALSO caught an over-correction I had missed after looking myself: the golden grade is past the point where hue distinctions survive.

What the critic asked for
  1. FIXED: the grade was multiplying EVERY near pixel by a warm colour - a global tint arriving through the post pass, the exact defect the lighting round existed to remove. Now weighted by each pixel's own luminance: full on lit surfaces, nothing in shadow.
  2. FIXED: the key's blue channel was closed. (1.0,0.70,0.42) at 2.4x turned grass albedo into yellow - NO lit surface could read green whatever its albedo.
  3. FIXED: HALF THE BLADE CARPET WAS RENDERING BLACK - three.js's DOUBLE_SIDED normal flip negating the blade's authored up-normal on back faces, with yaw uniform over 2pi, so ~half of 177k instances pointed at the dirt.
  4. FIXED: the grey rectangles were THE AO PASS'S OWN DEPTH BUFFER. GTAOPass draws its prepass with scene.overrideMaterial = MeshNormalMaterial, which carries neither the leaf atlas nor its alphaTest - so every alpha-cut foliage card writes its WHOLE QUAD into the g-buffer, and a camera-facing billboard is axis-aligned in screen space by construction. The aerial grade then read 11 m where the picture shows a 233 m ridge.
  5. FIXED: the 'unlit white trees' and the 'missing skybox' were ONE NUMBER - the fog was aimed at a colour between the two FURTHEST ridge rings while the terrain's skyline is drawn against ring 0.
  6. REFUSED: going all the way to the refs' near-band saturation was tried and the frame got worse - milky, flat trees, amber graded away. Stopped at 0.60-0.70 and said so.
