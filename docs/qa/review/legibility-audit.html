The question every shot is graded against is the user's: would a player who has never seen this scene know, from the image alone, where the entries and exits are and what route to take?

Nothing here is judged by eye alone. Each entry, exit and every metre of every route was measured in the running game by GL readback: a marker is drawn at the point with the depth test on, the frame is rendered, and the surviving pixels are counted against the shot's own baked depth map. 0% visible means the backdrop hides it — not that it looked hard to see. Two heights are reported because they are two questions: surface visibility (can a player see the path itself, before walking it) and figure visibility (can they at least follow their own character along it). The route polylines are derived from the town map's typed walk edges and the camera file's ownership, so they are the town's own statement of intent, not a guess (public/townmap/dellhollow.routes.json, tools/routes_derive.mjs).

One amendment to the rubric, made in the doing and flagged for the coordinator: fall-off is scored separately from the verdict. Almost every ribbon in this canyon town has an unguarded edge within 2 m — 91 places over 17 shots — so folding it into GREEN/AMBER/RED flattened the verdict and hid the legibility signal. The verdict is discoverability (entry-visible, exit-visible, exit-flow, route-visible, route-distinct); fall-off is its own ranked column and its own bucket-1 queue.

Redlines for coordinator-owned files

The audit found one systemic cause of off-frame exits: tools/cine_solve.mjs frames each shot around its owned region plus every arrival that lands in it — but not the seam centres it is an exit of. Every arrival is in frame (the solver guarantees it) while five exits are not. Proposed: add the at of every cut whose camFrom is this shot to the solver's sample set. That fixes all five by construction and protects every future town. It re-solves all cameras and needs a full re-bake, so it is the coordinator's call. Per-shot hand alternatives (aim height, metres, map coords) are in each card: lockhead −0.99, cottage −3.36, boatyard −1.93, waterfront −2.39.
