ONE-SURFACE DOMINANCE — the axis that won every placement sort, shipped

2026-08-09 · instrument tools/battle_surface.mjs · term PLACE.surf in public/js/battle_world.js · ?bsurf=0 (or --bsurf=0) is the one-build A/B

Three independent by-eye sorts of the same 62-site census agreed on which axis separates a good place to fight from a bad one: how much of the frame one surface owns — surf.topSurface and its histogram twin surf.entropy, at sep 0.844 on the round plate, 0.949 on the round plate against the decide sort and 0.937 on the decide frame. It beat sun exposure (which shipped instead), sky presence, view.back (what the solver already optimises) and the tonal metric (which runs backwards as a placement metric). It did not ship because it had no affordable proxy: a ray grid scored 0.79 and cost 52 ms a candidate against a ~42 ms solve.

1 · Why the ray grid failed, and it is visible in its own data

docs/qa/battle-placement/proxy-14x8.json reads meshTop ≥ 0.87 at every one of the 62 sites and exactly 1.00 at many of them, because the valley ground is ONE MESH. "One mesh owns the frame" is true everywhere. The winning axis was never about meshes — it is a statement about PIXELS, and it was always a colour histogram.

2 · The hypothesis tested first: can it come off a buffer TONE already produces?

TONE renders the arena into a 320×180 offscreen target twice per candidate boom; the without-cast pass IS that frame's background. At ladder time there is no cast at all, so the same measurement needs one render and no subtraction. The cost hypothesis was right and the quality hypothesis was wrong.

THE NEGATIVE RESULT IS THE FINDING: AN OFFSCREEN RENDER IS NOT A SMALL PICTURE OF THE GAME. The nearly-free version (2.0–2.8 ms, the buffer TONE already makes) tops out around sep 0.60 and ρ 0.63, at every resolution from 48×27 to 320×180, supersampled or not. Raising resolution and box-filtering down closed part of the gap (0.60 → 0.74 on the round sort) and none of the rest. The signal lives in the POST CHAIN: an offscreen target skips GTAO, bloom and the atmospheric grade, and those are what turn a far surface and a near one into two different colours. The page's own renderFrame() reaches sep 0.72–0.87 and ρ 0.85 for 7.3 ms.

And tone mapping is NOT the difference — checked, not assumed. The obvious suspect was the r185 rule that a non-XR render target renders in the LINEAR working space with OutputPass outside the loop. One explicit sRGB encode is applied to every offscreen byte measured here (--raw=1 measures the same frames without it), and ow-* ships NoToneMapping anyway, so there is no curve to miss. The gap is the post passes, not the transfer function.

THE ARMS ARE DETERMINISTIC AND THAT IS MEASURED, NOT ASSUMED. At the 39 sites the term did not move, surf.topSurface differs between the two arms by |Δ| p50 0.0002, max 0.0011, and zero sites differ by more than 0.01. Two things sit out every probe frame for the reasons TONE already wrote down — the PLAYER'S OWN BODY (staging runs before the fight hides it, so at ring 0 the player stands in the middle of every candidate frame) and AMBIENT'S MOTES (their boxes are centred 11 m down the LIVE camera's view axis). Both are visibility flips, never teardowns.

6 · Every frame that moved, looked at

Left of each pair is bsurf=0, right is bsurf=1. 19 clear wins, 3 washes (s008, s024, s046), 0 regressions by eye. The single objective regression, s012 at 0.213 → 0.222, is a frame that goes from the party standing in a pond to a village lawn — the ruler disagrees with the eye by 0.009 and the eye is right, which is what the ±10-point rater spread is about.

7 · Residuals, named

  • 15 sites still read ≥ 0.24 and 4 still read ≥ 0.40. The commonest reason is that the ladder has nothing better within reach: s061 has exactly ONE accepted candidate (0.484). A wider ladder is the next lever, and it costs candidate walk, not renders.
  • The surface term takes a shadier site at 3 of 23 moved sites (sun.shadedFrac mean 0.520 → 0.552). The two axes are not always aligned and the surface term is deliberately allowed to win, because it is the stronger axis on all three sorts — but this is the crack where a "sun is not contrast" style finding would appear next.
  • Ring 0 staging falls 29 → 15. The fight moves one sideways step more often than it did. The ruling's shape survives (every move is still a ring the ladder already reached) but "the fight stays where you are" is now the minority case.
  • The term optimises the ROUND establishing pose and the census measures the DECIDE frame. They agree directionally (that is what the decide-census lane established) but not exactly, which is where s012's 0.009 disagreement comes from.
  • The sky dome's cloud drift is still frozen at a wall-clock T and this probe does not touch it, exactly as TONE's residual says. It is inside the 0.0011 arm-to-arm noise measured above.
  • Not committed: the 124 per-site census jpgs (23 MB). The 23 that moved are in the sheets above; the 39 that did not are identical between arms to |Δtop| ≤ 0.0011. The JSON receipts (census-surf{Before,After}.json) are committed in full.
