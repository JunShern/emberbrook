Is the party's own albedo range wide enough to sit against this valley?

PARTY-ALBEDO lane · 2026-08-09 · 62-site census, both arms, real battles at the decide frame · instruments tools/battle_party.mjs, tools/battle_party_albedo.py, tools/battle_party_stats.py · measured with the shared meter tools/battle_meter.mjs, not a second ruler.

THE HYPOTHESIS DOES NOT SURVIVE. The party does not share this valley's paint any more than the creatures do. Party bodies overlap the terrain immediately around them at 0.5457 of their value histogram; the two foes — which bet H graded into the party's own band — overlap at 0.5276, and they fail to read 7/124 times against the party's 13/124. Overlap-with-terrain ranks a body that fails against one that reads at AUC 0.56 (party only: 0.547) — chance. And 40.1% of the variance in silhouette contrast is explained by WHICH SITE the fight is at, against 3.7% by WHICH BODY it is. A regrade of ratified characters is not indicated, and is not proposed.

6 · Why party bodies fail more often anyway — and it is the ruler's shape term, not their paint

edgeRGB is the magnitude of ONE signed difference of means over a whole silhouette. A body whose top is brighter than its surround and whose bottom is darker cancels itself toward zero. That is arithmetic, not a theory — and the two sides of this cast have opposite shapes: a standing person spans several surfaces top to bottom, a wolf or a blob sits on one. So the same contrast was cut into six horizontal bands over each body's own extent, which cannot cancel vertically.

THE SPLIT INVERTS. At the same census-wide failure rate, a ruler that cannot cancel vertically calls 7/124 party bodies and 13/124 foe bodies unreadable — the mirror image of edgeRGB's 13/124 and 7/124. Of the 13 party bodies edgeRGB calls failing, 12 have a 6-band contrast more than twice their own edgeRGB and 12 contain bands of opposite sign; their 6-band median is 14.48 against an edgeRGB median of 3.97. So the arc's premise — that legibility failures concentrate on small clothed party bodies — is substantially a statement about the ruler's shape term. This is a hand-off, not a shipped replacement: nothing has been eye-sorted under the new number.

8 · The proposal

Do not regrade the party. The evidence above says the intervention would not buy legibility at any strength a grade can carry, and it would spend a ratified character's appearance in towns, the overworld, every cut-in and every plate for it. That is a user call and this lane does not make it — but it is not a call worth putting to them on this evidence.

What the census says the remaining gap IS: the site. 40.1% of the variance in silhouette contrast is the site; 3.7% is the body. 13/62 sites have a party body the ruler calls failing, and at only 1 of those does a foe fail too. The lanes with the moving parts are placement quality (already shipped one refusal and one dominance term) and the ruler itself (§6).

What is on disk, and what is not

All 62 frames of the two-handed census are committed at full canvas resolution under census/pair/, because they are the evidence.
