The instrument this round needed, and did not have

The doctrine says "measure GROUND luminance on the region probes — the floor is what has to be read", and nothing in the tree could. plate_flat audits background leak, nav_eval asks an LLM, cine_visprobe needs Blender. tools/plate_probe.py (new, no Blender, no browser) reconstructs a world XYZ for every pixel out of the bundle's own two files — cine.json's solved camera and the rgb24-viewz depth.png — and derives a per-pixel normal from the world-position gradient, which splits the frame into GROUND / WALL / VOID. Three things it is careful about, each wrong once on the way here: depth is resampled NEAREST only (bg is 2688×1536, depth is 1344×768, and a filtered tap averages the packed bytes of two different depths); a depth discontinuity fakes a grazing normal, so a sample whose neighbour is >1.5 m away in world space is dropped rather than classified; and dark is not crushed — "crushed" means dark AND locally flat (L ≤ 24/255 with a 5×5 sd ≤ 2), because a dark surface that still carries texture is a shadow and one that carries none is a hole, and they want different fixes.

It names a REGION, never an object. A world box out of it is the INPUT to a Blender ray census. Round 3 has now three times recorded a worklist item whose named object was the wrong one; skipping that second step is how.

Fix — tools/dh_albedo_floor.py (new carrier). Raises every base colour in the measured black family to a FLOOR of 0.055, preserving hue (c ×= floor / L(c)): the flat mat_stone_black_cap, and the per-vertex Col of mat_blackstone and lf_matte — 18,660 loops over 65 objects, worst albedo before 0.00837. MATERIAL-SCOPED, and that is load-bearing: lock_four_dam is a joined mesh carrying eleven material slots over ONE shared Col layer whose median vertex luminance is 1.0, because mat_wallwood's loops are white there — a mesh-scoped lift would have repainted the lockhouse.

The gate is the ORDERING, asserted rather than assumed. 0.055 sits between charcoal (~0.04) and wet slate (~0.06–0.08), and the build FAILS if the family is not still the darkest thing in Dellhollow: measured floor 0.0550 < mat_timber_dark 0.1238, so locksfoot_build's art direction survives verbatim ("black … its coursing, its wet nappe and its boil, nothing more") while the surface stops being unrenderable. Idempotent, and the second run does not rewrite the receipt (a no-op that overwrote the manifest with an empty one was caught and fixed here — a receipt that lies is worse than no receipt).

The claim, verbatim from the round-3 handover

Refutation 1 — "no Principled BSDF" is the design, not a defect

mat_haze_east is a two-node Volume Scatter (0.48/0.50/0.60, anisotropy 0.3) with no surface shader — and so are mat_haze_far, _mid, _rim, _south and mat_spray. An albedo resolver that walks the tree looking for a surface BSDF returns nothing on all six. It found its own blind spot, not a broken material.

Refutation 2 — the card is an ATTENUATOR, and the census that named it did not march through it

New instrument tools/dh_pixel_census.py. It models the BEAUTY pass rather than the ray-caster's world: objects with hide_render are dropped, and a render-only volume card (a material with a Volume link and no Surface link) is MARCHED THROUGH, accumulating tau = density × path, until the ray lands on something that actually shades. Both halves are failures this repo has already written down — "a first-hit tally that does not drop hide_render objects is a lie about the frame" and "VOLUME CARDS ARE MESHES TO A RAY-CASTER".

The fix, and its class — ATMOSPHERE, not light and not grade

Nothing is added, moved or re-energised, and no exposure or view transform is touched, so the town's lighting doctrine ("adjusting an existing light has never moved this town; ADDING a source always has") is deliberately not the governing rule. More light makes a tiling artefact legible; more haze hides it AND lifts the black. That is why the haze is the correct lever and not merely the available one.
