# Prop wishlist

Gaps found while transcribing full-scene CONCEPT IMAGES onto the grid with the
real catalog. Each entry: what the concept composition wanted, the nearest
catalog equivalent used instead, and why a dedicated asset would help.

## THE SQUARE — concept-driven layout pilot (2026-07-28)

Concept authority: `public/assets/iso/refs/square-concept-B.png` (Emberwake
festival square). Substitutions logged during the rebuild of `scenes/square.json`:

- **Festoon / string-lights garland** (glowing bulb strings strung post-to-post
  and across the plaza) — the concept drapes warm string-lights all along the top
  and side edges to frame the square and knit the posts together. Nearest owned:
  `bunting-post` / `bunting-post2` (triangular cloth flags only, no lights). A
  spanning festoon-light garland is the single biggest missing festival dressing
  piece; bunting alone reads flatter and doesn't carry the dusk glow.
- **Loose pumpkin / gourd ground pile** — the concept heaps pumpkins and squashes
  directly on the earth beside the stalls and by the townhouse. Nearest owned:
  `f-pumpkincart` (pumpkins already loaded in a cart). Substituted the cart in
  both harvest clusters; a free-standing gourd heap would let a cluster read as
  "spilling produce" rather than always "another cart."
- **Hanging flower basket (lamppost-mounted)** — the concept hangs flower baskets
  and lanterns off the lampposts and the big tree. Owned `flower-cart` / `planter`
  sit on the ground only; the tree already shows tie-on lanterns but there is no
  basket that mounts to a `lamppost`. Used ground planters as the nearest stand-in.
- **Festival banner / entry arch** — the concept spans a welcome banner across the
  north processional road into the square. No arch/banner prop exists; framed the
  entrance with paired `bunting-post` + `lamppost` gateway instead.
- **Small standing ring-lanterns** — the concept rings the plinth with low ornate
  standing lanterns at tight spacing. Approximated with alternating `f-brazier`
  (fire bowls) + tall `lamppost` at the ring corners; a short decorative
  ring-lantern would let the sacred ring read denser without four full lampposts.
