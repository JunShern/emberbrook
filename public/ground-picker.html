BLEND MODE — the material boundaries themselves

The director flagged material-to-material blending as a current problem. A selectable blend mode is now in the engine (?blend=0|1 in the proto, or __isoBlend(m)). Every candidate row above is rendered with the improved blend (1). Below, the same village baseline map at each mode so you can see the change on its own: current (0) is a tight soft feather that can read as a rubber halo; improved (1) widens the feather and breaks the edge into a noise-dithered, hand-stippled organic border. Watch the grass→cobble edge.
